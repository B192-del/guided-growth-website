About your guide

18 years building products. Several years deep in AI. One mission now.

I've spent the last 18 years launching products — the kind of work that forces you to translate new, confusing technology into something real people can actually use. For the last several years, that technology has been AI, and I've been learning and using it hands-on the whole way through.

Here's what I keep running into: most people have heard of ChatGPT, but have no real idea how to use it meaningfully or responsibly. And it cuts both ways — teenagers in particular often detest AI outright, not because they've tried it and found it lacking, but because of misinformation that's never been corrected for them.

My mission with Guided Growth is to clear that up for everyone, at whatever level they're starting from: show people what's actually possible with AI, and how to use it well and responsibly. AI itself changes constantly, so these courses aren't static — I update them as the tools and best practices evolve, so what you learn stays current, not outdated the moment you finish.
